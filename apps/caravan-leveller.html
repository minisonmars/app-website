Caravan Leveller

A bubble level for your caravan that tells you exactly how much to raise each side.

Put the phone flat in the caravan, point it at the hitch, and it tells you what to do, for example Raise LEFT side 4.00 cm or Lower FRONT 3.50 cm.

What it does

  • A smooth bubble level that turns green within 0.5°.
  • Side-to-side and front-to-back tilt in degrees.
  • Raise and lower amounts in cm or inches, worked out from your caravan's track width and hitch-to-axle length.
  • One round bubble, or two separate vials for side to side and front to back.
  • Several vehicles (caravan, campervan, motorhome, tow car), each with its own sizes, weights, photo, details and calibration. Many Swift, Coachman and Bailey caravans can be filled in from their published figures.
  • Setting-up, packing-away and packing checklists, with the kit needed for driving in European countries added for the countries you choose.
  • Service records with photos and files, and reminders by date or mileage with notifications, optionally shown in your phone calendar.
  • A compass with sunrise and sunset directions, the nearest TV transmitters, satellite dish pointing and your mobile signal.
  • A trip diary of every site you stay at, with ratings, photos and contact details, searchable and shown on a map.
  • Your choice of colour, in light or dark.
  • Backup to a file you keep wherever you like, and restore from it.
  • Optional spoken instructions, so you can hear what to do while you wind the jockey wheel.
  • Works offline.

Get the app

The app is not on Google Play yet. You can install the latest version from its releases page on GitHub.
