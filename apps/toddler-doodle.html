Toddler Doodle

A simple drawing app for toddlers, with big buttons and a PIN-protected parent area.

A big sheet of paper, ten bright colours and buttons that are hard to press by accident.

What it does

  • Ten colours in one scrolling line, with an eraser at the start.
  • Several fingers can draw at once.
  • Undo, and a Clear button that has to be held for a second.
  • Save to the app's own gallery, which only ever shows saved doodles, never the device's photos.
  • Parent area (hold the gear, then enter the PIN): turn Save, Eraser and Gallery on or off, look at or delete doodles, and copy them to Photos.

It works with Toddler Launcher, and can be installed from the launcher's parent area.

Get the app

The app is not on Google Play yet. You can install the latest version from its releases page on GitHub.
