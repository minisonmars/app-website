Toddler Camera

A stripped-down camera for toddlers: big buttons, nothing to get lost in.

Photos and videos with the front or back camera, and a simple gallery. No zoom, no settings and no delete button for little fingers.

What it does

  • One big button to take a picture or start and stop a video.
  • A big-tile gallery with arrows to move between pictures and videos.
  • Pictures and videos are saved to the device, so they also appear in the normal gallery app.
  • Parent settings behind a PIN: switch photo, video and gallery on or off, picture size, flash, fun shutter sounds, video length, screen pinning and a play time limit.

Get the app

The app is not on Google Play yet. You can install the latest version from its releases page on GitHub.
